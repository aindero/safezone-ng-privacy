01

Who We Are

SafeZone NG is operated by BigBite Intercontinental Ltd, a company incorporated in Nigeria (RC No. 1573129), with its registered address at No. 5 Lewis Aindero Street, Egan Igando, Alimosho, Lagos, Nigeria.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal data when you use the SafeZone NG mobile application. It applies to all users of the app, regardless of location.

We are committed to compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019, the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, and — where applicable to users in the European Economic Area — the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Minimum age: SafeZone NG is intended for users aged 18 years and above. We do not knowingly collect data from persons under 18. If you become aware that a minor has created an account, please contact us immediately.

02

Data We Collect

Account & Identity Data

  • Mobile phone number (used for OTP authentication via Firebase Authentication)
  • Display name (optional, user-provided)
  • Profile photo (optional, user-provided)

Location Data

  • Foreground location — collected while the app is open and in use, to display nearby security alerts, power the map view, and enable safe routing.
  • Background location (Silent Tracker Mode only) — when you voluntarily activate Silent Tracker Mode (a kidnap-tracking feature), the app collects your GPS location continuously in the background for up to 72 hours, then automatically stops. This requires explicit activation by you and cannot be enabled remotely.

Incident Reports

  • Text descriptions of reported incidents
  • Photos captured or uploaded via the app (stored in Firebase Storage)
  • Voice notes (stored in Firebase Storage)
  • Location at time of report
  • Incident category and timestamp

Incident reports are submitted anonymously by default. We do not attach your identity to the public-facing report unless you choose to identify yourself.

Emergency Contact Data

  • Names and phone numbers of trusted contacts you add for SOS alerts
  • This data is stored in Firestore and used solely to send SOS notifications on your behalf

Device & Technical Data

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token — used to deliver push notifications
  • Device type, OS version (collected by Firebase automatically)
  • App interaction logs (Firestore read/write operations)

Payment Data

  • Subscription payments are processed via the Paystack in-app SDK
  • We do not store card numbers or bank details. Payment data is handled directly by Paystack under their privacy policy
  • We retain your subscription status and transaction reference in Firestore

06

Your Rights

Under the NDPR, NDPA 2023, and GDPR (where applicable), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right of Access

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

Right to Rectification

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

Right to Portability

Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

Right to Object

Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent (e.g. Silent Tracker) at any time without penalty.

Right to Restrict Processing

Request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

File a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) or your local supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at bigbiteintercontinental@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Rights Request." We will respond within 30 days.

[REVIEW] Account deletion is currently a manual process. Implement an in-app "Delete Account" button that triggers a Cloud Function to purge Firestore documents, Firebase Auth record, Storage files, and FCM tokens — ideally within 30 days of request.
07

Security

We implement the following measures to protect your data:

  • All data transmitted between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit via TLS/HTTPS
  • Firebase Security Rules restrict Firestore and Storage access to authenticated users only
  • Phone OTP authentication reduces the risk of unauthorised account access
  • Cloud Functions run in Google's secured infrastructure (us-central1 region)
  • Silent Tracker location data is scoped to trusted contacts only and not publicly accessible

No system is perfectly secure. In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify affected users and the NDPC within 72 hours of becoming aware, in accordance with NDPA 2023 Section 40.

08

Children's Privacy

SafeZone NG is not directed at persons under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has registered on the platform, please contact us at bigbiteintercontinental@gmail.com and we will promptly delete their data.
