# SafeZone NG Privacy Policy

**Last updated: April 2026**

SafeZone NG is operated by SafeZone NG.

## Information We Collect
- **Location data** — used to display nearby security alerts
- **Camera access** — used for incident photo reporting
- **Phone number** — used for authentication and SOS alerts
- **Device identifiers** — used for push notification delivery

## How We Use Your Information
Collected data is used solely to provide app functionality.
We do not sell your data to third parties.

## Data Retention
User data is retained only as long as necessary to provide services.
